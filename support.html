Frequently asked questions

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

No. AllFun is an independent website, not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible.

Do supporters get extra games?

No — every game stays free for everyone. Supporters get a thank-you on our Supporters wall (if they want it), a supporter badge in Friday Fun and a vote on which game we build next.

How do I pay?

If instant checkout buttons appear above, use those. Otherwise send a pledge and we'll email a secure payment link within one business day.

Can I cancel a monthly contribution?

Anytime. Reply to any receipt or use the contact page and we'll stop it immediately.

Can I direct my gift?

Yes — choose where it goes in the form. We'll report how earmarked support was spent.
