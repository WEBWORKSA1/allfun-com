Why a printed kit?

Phones are great for timers and scoreboards, but paper keeps everyone in the room. The kit is designed to print on standard letter or A4 paper: one page of rules per few games, a reusable score sheet and a bingo card that turns the whole evening into a meta-game. Mix in our charades generator and team picker for the digital bits.
