How streaks work

Complete at least one daily challenge — FiveWord, the Daily Sudoku or the Daily 5 — on a given day and your streak grows by one. Miss a day and it starts again. Streaks are saved in your browser on this device, so there's nothing to sign up for (and clearing your browser data will reset them).
