# AllFun.com: Market Research

Compiled 2026-10-29. Every figure below comes from a page retrieved during this research. All traffic and keyword numbers are third-party **estimates** (Semrush, Similarweb, Ahrefs, seodata.dev). They are not first-party analytics. Different tools often disagree, sometimes by 3x or more, so read them as orders of magnitude. "Not found" means no retrieved page gave the figure.

**Patterns:**
- **Quiz and game sites lead on depth.** Sporcle, JetPunk, chess.com and neal.fun reach 6 to 18 pages per visit.
- **Single-purpose tools get time but not depth.** Wheel and coin tools show 1.2 to 2.4 pages per visit and 64–85% bounce.
- **Fact pages are shallow.** Fact sites average under 2 pages and about 1 minute per visit.
- **Semrush durations run 1.5 to 2x Similarweb's.** For teambuilding.com the gap is 06:06 against 00:23.

Sporcle's Google publisher story (2023) cites about 100M pageviews a month and more than 4B quiz plays by 2021. [R8]
