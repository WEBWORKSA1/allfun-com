Frequently asked questions

Is Plan My Fun really free?

Yes. Planning ideas and introductions to partners are free for you. If you choose to book a partner, you deal with them directly and AllFun may receive a referral fee from that partner — it never changes your price.

How fast will I hear back?

Within one business day, usually sooner. Urgent date? Mention it in your notes.

Do you run the events yourselves?

For small groups we send a ready-to-run plan you can host yourself. For larger or professionally facilitated events, we match you with vetted planners, venues and hosts.

Who sees my details?

AllFun reviews every request. With your consent, we share it with up to three relevant partners so they can send ideas or quotes. We never sell your details to marketers.

Can you help with virtual or international teams?

Yes. Many of our ideas work over any video-call app and across time zones.
