Make fun for a living (or a weekend)

AllFun is a small, remote-first studio. We hire freelancers and contractors around the world to build games, write puzzles, draw, moderate and grow the community.

Open roles

🕹️ HTML5 Game Developer

Contract · Remote

Build small, polished browser games in vanilla JavaScript or a lightweight framework. You care about feel, performance on cheap phones and accessibility.

We look for: A portfolio of playable web games; clean, dependency-light code.

🧩 Puzzle & Quiz Writer

Freelance · Remote

Write trivia sets, riddles, word puzzles and personality quizzes that are accurate, fair and genuinely fun.

We look for: Writing samples and a love of fact-checking.

🎨 Illustrator / Motion Designer

Freelance · Remote

Create characters, game art and small animations in AllFun's bright, sticker-book style.

We look for: A portfolio with playful character and UI work.

🛡️ Community & Contest Moderator

Part-time · Remote

Review contest entries, verify high scores, answer players and keep everything friendly and fair.

We look for: Good judgment, clear writing, a few hours a week.

🎬 Video Creator / Editor

Freelance · Remote

Make short videos of game tips, puzzle walkthroughs and party ideas for social channels.

We look for: Short-form editing samples.

🤝 Partnerships & Sponsorship Lead

Commission · Remote

Bring brands, event partners and prize sponsors to AllFun.

We look for: Experience selling sponsorships or media.
