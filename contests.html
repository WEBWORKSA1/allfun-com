Official rules

1. Sponsor and eligibility

Contests are run by AllFun.com ("AllFun"). Open to individuals worldwide where not prohibited by law. Entrants under the age of majority in their place of residence must have permission from a parent or legal guardian, who must accept any prize on their behalf. Employees and contractors of AllFun and their immediate households may not win. Void where prohibited or restricted.

2. No purchase necessary

No purchase, payment or donation is necessary to enter or win. Supporting AllFun does not improve your chances of winning.

3. How to enter

Use the entry form on this page during the open round (the calendar month). Limit one entry per person per contest per round. Entries must be your own original, family-friendly work and must not infringe anyone's rights. Automated or bulk entries are disqualified.

4. Judging

Winners are selected on skill or merit using the criteria shown for each contest — not by chance. High-score entries must be verifiable; AllFun may request additional proof. Judges' decisions are final.

5. Prizes

Prizes are as described for each contest. Prizes are non-transferable; no cash alternative unless AllFun chooses to offer one. AllFun may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes. Where required by local law, a skill-testing question may need to be answered correctly before a prize is awarded.

6. Winner notification

Winners are notified by email within 14 days after the round closes and must reply within 14 days, or an alternate winner may be selected.

7. License and publicity

By entering, you give AllFun a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display your entry and display name on AllFun and its channels in connection with the contest. You keep ownership of your entry.

8. Privacy and general conditions

Entry information is used to run the contest as described in our privacy policy. AllFun may cancel, modify or suspend a contest if it cannot run as planned, and may disqualify entries that break these rules. These rules are subject to applicable law.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay to enter?

No. Every AllFun contest is free to enter. No purchase is necessary and buying anything does not improve your chances.

How are winners chosen?

By skill or by a judging panel using the published criteria — never by random draw.

When are winners announced?

Within 14 days after each monthly round closes, on this page and by email to the winners.

Can kids enter?

Entrants under the age of majority where they live need a parent or guardian's permission, and the parent or guardian accepts the prize on their behalf.

Can my company sponsor a prize?

Yes! Sponsors get logo placement on this page and in Friday Fun. See Advertise & sponsor.
