What is AllFun?

AllFun is a free, family-friendly playground for the internet. We build original browser games, write trivia and personality quizzes, make simple tools for parties and classrooms, and publish practical guides to having more fun — with friends, family, coworkers or on your own. Everything runs in your browser and saves progress on your device, so there's no account to create.
