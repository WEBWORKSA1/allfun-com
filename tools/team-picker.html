Random Team Generator: fair teams in one click

Paste names, choose team count or size, get balanced random teams.

Share:

Paste names, pick how many teams (or how many people per team), and get fair, random teams instantly. Turn on fun team names for extra energy at game nights, classrooms and office events.

How to use the team picker

  1. Enter one name per line.
  2. Choose 'number of teams' or 'people per team' and set the number.
  3. Press Make teams. Press again to reshuffle.
  4. Use Copy teams to paste the result anywhere.

Ideas and tips

  • Teams stay balanced within one person of each other.
  • Remix until no team ends up with all the competitive players — we won't judge.
  • Save time by keeping the list; it's remembered on this device.
  • Pair with the spin wheel to decide which team goes first.

Frequently asked questions

Are the teams random?

Yes. Names are shuffled using your browser's secure random generator.

Is my list saved?

Only in your browser, so it's there next time.

What if numbers don't divide evenly?

Extra people are spread across teams so sizes differ by at most one.

How many names can I add?

As many as you like — it works for big events too.
