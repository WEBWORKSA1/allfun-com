Dice Roller: roll virtual dice online

Roll 1–6 dice with 4 to 20 sides. Totals and roll history.

Share:

Lost the dice again? Roll up to six virtual dice with 4 to 20 sides each. Totals are added for you, and your last dozen rolls stay listed below.

How to use the dice roller

  1. Choose how many dice and how many sides.
  2. Press Roll (or the R key).
  3. Six-sided dice show pips; others show numbers.
  4. Your recent rolls appear in the history.

Ideas and tips

  • Use two six-sided dice for most family board games.
  • Teachers: roll two dice for instant math facts practice.
  • A 20-sided die is great for 'pick a number' moments.
  • Press Space or R for quick rolls during a game.

Frequently asked questions

Are the rolls random?

Yes, each die uses your browser's secure random generator.

Which dice can I roll?

Four, six, eight, ten, twelve and twenty sides.

Can I roll more than six dice?

Six at once keeps it readable; roll again for more.

Does it work offline?

Once the page has loaded, rolling works without a connection.
