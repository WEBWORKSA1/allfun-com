Bored Button: press for something fun to do

One press, one idea. Filter by mood, cost, time and company.

Share:

Two hundred hand-picked ideas, one big button. Filter by what you're in the mood for, how much time you have, what it costs and who's around — then press and go. Some ideas link straight to a game or tool here.

How to use the bored button

  1. Set any filters you like (or leave them open).
  2. Press the button for a random idea.
  3. Press again for another — you won't see repeats until the list runs out.
  4. Ideas with a 'Do it on AllFun' button open the matching game or tool.

Ideas and tips

  • Pick 'Free' and '15 min' for a quick reset between tasks.
  • With kids, choose 'Family' and 'Creative' for craft-friendly ideas.
  • Can't choose between two? Put them on the spin wheel.
  • Keep a running list of ideas you loved for rainy days.

Frequently asked questions

How many ideas are there?

200, across eight categories from Creative to Kitchen.

Are the kitchen ideas safe for kids?

They're simple, and any idea involving heat or sharp tools notes that an adult should help.

Do the ideas cost money?

Most are free or low-cost. Use the cost filter to be sure.

Can I suggest an idea?

Yes — send it through the contact page.
