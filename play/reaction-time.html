A simple test with a surprisingly competitive streak. Wait for the box to turn green, then tap or press Space as fast as you can. After five rounds you get your average in milliseconds.

How to play Reaction Time Test

  1. Tap the box to start.
  2. Wait while it's pink — tapping early resets the round.
  3. When it turns green, tap immediately.
  4. Your average of five rounds is your score.

Tips and strategy

  • Rest your finger lightly on the screen or key — no big movement needed.
  • Look at the center of the box, not the edges.
  • Your device matters: wired mice and high-refresh screens shave milliseconds.
  • Take three warm-up rounds before going for a record.

About the game

Simple visual reaction tests are used in classrooms to teach measurement and in sports training as a fun benchmark. Results vary with device latency, so compare scores on the same device.

Frequently asked questions

What's a typical result?

Many people score somewhere around 200–300 ms on a test like this; device and screen add some delay.

Why did I get 'Too soon'?

You tapped before the box turned green. That round restarts.

Does my phone make me slower?

Touchscreens and some displays add a little latency, so compare scores on the same device.

Is my best saved?

Yes — your best five-round average is stored locally.
