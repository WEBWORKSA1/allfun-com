Get the kit

The arcade classic, rebuilt for keyboards and phones. Every star you eat makes you longer and a little faster. Grab the bonus gem before it vanishes for big points. By default you wrap around the edges; switch on solid walls for the hardcore version.

How to play Snake

  1. Press Play, then steer with arrow keys, WASD, swipes or the on-screen pad.
  2. Eat stars for 10 points each — you grow by one segment.
  3. Bonus gems are worth 50 but disappear quickly.
  4. Running into your own body ends the game. Space pauses.

Tips and strategy

  • Hug the edges early to keep the middle open for later.
  • Move in long zig-zag lanes when you're long — it keeps an escape route free.
  • Don't chase a gem if it means trapping yourself.
  • Turn on solid walls only once you're comfortable — it removes the wrap-around safety net.

About the game

Snake-style games appeared in arcades in the 1970s and later became famous on early mobile phones. The rules have barely changed because they don't need to.

Frequently asked questions

Does the speed increase?

Yes, slightly with every star, up to a cap.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes — swipe on the board or use the arrow pad below it.

What are solid walls?

With solid walls on, touching the edge ends the game instead of wrapping you around.

Is my high score saved?

Yes, locally in your browser.
