Get the kit

Four colored pads, each with its own tone. The game plays a pattern; you play it back. Every correct round adds one more step. How long a sequence can you hold in your head?

How to play Pattern Echo

  1. Press Start and watch the first pad light up.
  2. Repeat the sequence by tapping the pads or pressing 1–4.
  3. Each round adds one step to the end.
  4. Normal mode replays the pattern after a mistake; Strict mode ends the game.

Tips and strategy

  • Hum the tones — sound memory backs up visual memory.
  • Chunk long patterns into groups of three or four.
  • Say the colors in your head as they flash.
  • If you're a visual learner, picture the pads as a clock: top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right.

About the game

Repeat-the-pattern games are a classic way to test working memory, the short-term 'scratchpad' you use to hold information for a few seconds. Many people can hold around seven items before it gets hard.

Frequently asked questions

Can I turn off the sound?

Yes, use the Sound button. Your choice is remembered.

What does Strict mode do?

One mistake ends the game. Normal mode just replays the pattern.

What's a good level?

Reaching level 10 is solid; 15+ is impressive.

Is it accessible by keyboard?

Yes — keys 1–4 map to the four pads.
