Get the kit

Drop discs into a seven-column grid. Get four of your color in a line — across, up, or diagonally — before your opponent does. Play the computer at three difficulty levels or pass the device to a friend.

How to play Four in a Row

  1. Tap a column to drop your yellow disc to the lowest open space.
  2. Players alternate turns.
  3. Line up four in a row horizontally, vertically or diagonally to win.
  4. If the board fills with no line, it's a draw.

Tips and strategy

  • Claim the center column — it's part of more possible lines than any other.
  • Watch for threats your opponent builds two moves ahead.
  • Create two threats at once; your opponent can only block one.
  • Think about which row each threat sits on — odd and even rows matter late in the game.

About the game

Vertical four-in-a-row games have been played for decades, and the standard 7×6 board has been studied deeply by mathematicians. Our computer opponent looks several moves ahead and gets sharper on Hard.

Frequently asked questions

How strong is the Hard computer?

It searches six moves ahead and rarely misses a tactic. Beat it and you've earned bragging rights.

Who goes first?

You always play yellow and move first against the computer.

Can two people play?

Yes. Choose Friend (same device) and take turns.

Are wins tracked?

Your record against the computer is saved in your browser.
