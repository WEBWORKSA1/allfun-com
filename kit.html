Your AllFun Game Night Kit

Print it, bookmark it, share it with your co-host. Have a brilliant night.

Open the charades generatorMake teams

Welcome to the AllFun Game Night Kit! Everything here is designed to get a group laughing fast, whether you have three players or thirteen, a full evening or just one hour after dinner. Most games need nothing more than paper, pens, and a timer. Print this kit, pick five or six games that suit your crowd, and keep the score sheet and bingo card handy. The golden rule: if everyone is laughing, you are doing it right.

Host checklist

  1. Pick a start time and a rough end time so everyone knows the plan.
  2. Choose five or six games in advance, plus two backups in case one falls flat.
  3. Mix it up: start with a quick icebreaker, add a team game, and finish with something calm.
  4. Gather supplies: paper, pens, a timer, a bowl or hat, and a few sticky notes.
  5. Print one score sheet and one bingo card per player or team.
  6. Clear a space for active games and move anything breakable out of the way.
  7. Set out easy snacks in small bowls so nobody has to leave the fun for long.
  8. Fill a pitcher of water or juice and stack cups nearby.
  9. Explain each game in under a minute, then play a practice round.
  10. Split teams fairly by mixing ages, skill levels, and loud and quiet players.
  11. Name a friendly referee for each game to settle questions quickly.
  12. End on a high note with prizes, a group photo, and a vote for next time's favorite.

30 game-night games

1. Forbidden Words

Players: 4+ in two teams · Time: 20 min · You need: Index cards, timer

Write a target word on each card with three related words underneath that may not be said. One player describes the target word to their team in 60 seconds without using any forbidden words. The other team watches for slip-ups. Each correct guess earns one point.

2. Who Am I?

Players: 3–12 · Time: 15 min · You need: Sticky notes, pens

Everyone writes a famous animal, character type, or job on a sticky note and secretly sticks it on another player's forehead. Players take turns asking yes-or-no questions to figure out who they are. The first to guess correctly wins, but keep playing until everyone knows.

3. Two Truths and a Fib

Players: 3+ · Time: 15 min · You need: Nothing

Each player shares three short statements about themselves: two true and one made up. Everyone else votes on which one is the fib. Fool the majority and you score a point. Great for groups who do not know each other well yet.

4. Three-Round Hat Game

Players: 6+ in two teams · Time: 30 min · You need: Paper slips, a hat or bowl, timer

Everyone writes five nouns on slips and drops them in the hat. In round one, describe the words freely. In round two, use only one word as a clue. In round three, act them out silently. The same slips are used every round, so memory becomes a secret weapon.

5. Classic Charades

Players: 4+ in two teams · Time: 20–30 min · You need: Word slips or a charades generator, timer

One player acts out a word or phrase without speaking while their team guesses within one minute. Agree on simple signals first, like holding up fingers for the number of words. Swap teams after every turn and keep a running tally.

6. Hum That Tune

Players: 3+ · Time: 15 min · You need: Nothing

One player hums a well-known song, such as a lullaby, holiday tune, or birthday song, with no words allowed. The first person to name it scores a point and hums next. Keep songs familiar so younger players can join in.

7. Categories Countdown

Players: 2–10 · Time: 15 min · You need: Paper, pens, timer

Pick a letter and list six categories, such as fruits, cities, and animals. Players get two minutes to write one answer per category starting with that letter. Unique answers earn two points, while answers shared with someone else earn one point.

8. One-Word Story

Players: 3+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Nothing

Sit in a circle and build a story one word at a time, with each player adding just one word. Try to keep the story making sense. If someone hesitates for more than three seconds, they start a new sentence. Laughter is basically guaranteed.

9. Alphabet Chain

Players: 2+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Nothing

Choose a category, like foods or animals. The first player names something starting with A, the next with B, and so on through the alphabet. Get stuck for five seconds and you are out for the round. Skip tricky letters like X if you like.

10. Rhyme Time

Players: 2+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Nothing

Say a simple word, such as "cat." Going around the circle, each player says a new word that rhymes. Repeat a word or freeze for five seconds and you sit out. The last rhymer standing picks the next starting word.

11. Mystery Word Snowman

Players: 2+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Paper, pen

One player picks a secret word and draws a blank for each letter. Guessers call out one letter at a time. Correct letters fill in the blanks, while wrong guesses add a piece to a snowman. Solve the word before the snowman gets a hat, scarf, and carrot nose.

12. Fortunately, Unfortunately

Players: 3+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Nothing

The first player starts a story with one sentence. The next player continues with a sentence beginning "Fortunately…" and the next with "Unfortunately…" Keep alternating as the story twists and turns in ridiculous directions.

13. Word Ladder

Players: 1–6 · Time: 15 min · You need: Paper, pens

Pick two four-letter words, like "cold" and "warm." Change one letter at a time to make a new real word at each step until you reach the target word. The player who finishes in the fewest steps wins the round.

14. Pass the Doodle

Players: 5–12 · Time: 20 min · You need: Stapled paper booklets, pens

Each player writes a silly phrase on the first page and passes the booklet left. The next player draws it, folds back the phrase, and passes again. The next player writes a guess based only on the drawing. Keep alternating, then reveal every booklet to see how wildly the ideas changed.

15. Speed Sketch

Players: 4+ in two teams · Time: 20 min · You need: Paper or whiteboard, word slips, timer

One player draws a secret word while their team guesses within 60 seconds. No letters, numbers, or talking allowed for the artist. Correct guesses score a point. This is the classic drawing-guessing game, and stick figures count as art.

16. Paper Plate Portraits

Players: 3+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Paper plates, markers

Everyone holds a paper plate on top of their head and draws a portrait of the person on their right without looking. Add a nose, eyes, mouth, hair, and ears one at a time as the host calls them out. Reveal together and vote for the funniest.

17. Back-to-Back Drawing

Players: 2+ in pairs · Time: 15 min · You need: Paper, pens, simple picture cards

Partners sit back to back. One describes a simple picture using only shapes and directions, like "draw a big circle in the middle." The other draws it without peeking. Compare the results and switch roles.

18. Trivia Showdown

Players: 4+ in teams · Time: 20–30 min · You need: Trivia questions, paper, pens

Read questions aloud and give teams 30 seconds to write their answer. Reveal answers after each round of five questions. Use the tie-breaker trivia in this kit for close games, and give a bonus point for the most creative wrong answer.

19. Silent Line-Up

Players: 6+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Nothing

Without speaking, the group must line up in order of birthday, from January to December. Players can use gestures and fingers only. When done, check the order out loud. Try again with height, shoe size, or the first letter of first names.

20. Sixty-Second Challenges

Players: 2+ · Time: 20 min · You need: Paper cups, cotton balls, a spoon, timer

Set up quick tasks: stack ten cups into a pyramid, move cotton balls between bowls with a spoon, or keep a feather in the air by blowing. Each player gets one minute per challenge. Score a point for every challenge completed in time.

21. Would You Rather Showdown

Players: 3+ · Time: 15 min · You need: A list of would-you-rather questions

Read a question and have everyone point left or right at the same time to pick their side. The smaller side gets 30 seconds to convince the others to switch. Score a point for every player you persuade.

22. Freeze Dance

Players: 3+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Music

Play upbeat music while everyone dances. When the music stops, everyone freezes. Anyone who wobbles or giggles sits out and becomes a judge. Last dancer moving wins. Try themed rounds, like dancing as robots or in slow motion.

23. Balloon Keep-Up

Players: 2+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Balloons

Work as a team to keep one balloon from touching the floor. Count every tap out loud. For a challenge, add a second balloon or allow only elbows and heads. Try to beat your record each round.

24. Cup Stack Relay

Players: 6+ in two teams · Time: 15 min · You need: 10 paper cups per team

Teams line up across the room from their cups. One at a time, players run over, stack the cups into a pyramid, unstack them, and race back to tag the next teammate. The first team to finish wins.

25. Spoon Scramble

Players: 4–8 · Time: 15 min · You need: Deck of cards, spoons (one fewer than players)

Place spoons in the middle and deal four cards to each player. Pass cards around quickly, trying to collect four of a kind. When someone does, they quietly grab a spoon, and everyone else scrambles to grab one too. The player left without a spoon gets a letter; spell "SPOON" and you are out.

26. Dots and Boxes

Players: 2–4 · Time: 15 min · You need: Paper, pens

Draw a grid of dots. Players take turns drawing a single line between two dots. Complete the fourth side of a box and you write your initial inside it and take another turn. Most boxes when the grid fills wins.

27. Twenty Questions

Players: 2+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Nothing

One player thinks of an object, animal, or place. Everyone else asks yes-or-no questions to figure it out, with a limit of 20 questions total. Guess correctly to become the next thinker.

28. Captain Says

Players: 3+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Nothing

The leader gives action commands like "Captain says touch your nose." Players follow only when the command starts with "Captain says." Anyone who follows a command without it sits out. Let kids take turns as the leader.

29. I Spy Colors

Players: 2+ · Time: 10 min · You need: Nothing

One player says, "I spy something…" and names its color. Others take turns guessing what it is. Correct guessers become the spy. For older players, use shapes, textures, or first letters instead of colors.

30. Sardines

Players: 4+ · Time: 20 min · You need: A safe indoor space

This is hide-and-seek in reverse. One player hides while everyone else counts to 50. Seekers search, and when they find the hider, they squeeze into the hiding spot too. The last seeker to find the group hides next. Agree on off-limits rooms first.

Scoring & prizes

Keep scoring simple so the fun stays front and center.

  • One point per win. Award one point to each winning player or team in every game. Highest total at the end wins the night.
  • Bonus points. Give one extra point for the best sportsmanship, funniest answer, or most dramatic performance of the round.
  • Comeback rule. In the final round, double all points so every team stays in the running.
  • Ties. Break ties with a single question from the tie-breaker trivia below.

Fun prizes that cost little or nothing:

  • A handmade paper crown or medal for the overall champion
  • The right to choose the first game at the next game night
  • First pick of snacks or dessert
  • A "no dishes tonight" pass
  • A silly trophy made from a decorated cup or jar
  • A homemade certificate, like "Master of Charades" or "Champion Doodler"
  • Control of the music playlist for the rest of the evening

25 tie-breaker trivia questions

  1. How many legs does a spider have? (Eight)
  2. What is the largest planet in our solar system? (Jupiter)
  3. How many continents are there on Earth? (Seven)
  4. What is the largest ocean on Earth? (Pacific Ocean)
  5. Which planet is known as the Red Planet? (Mars)
  6. How many sides does a hexagon have? (Six)
  7. What is the hardest natural substance on Earth? (Diamond)
  8. At what temperature does water freeze in degrees Fahrenheit? (32 degrees)
  9. How many bones are in the adult human body? (206)
  10. What is the tallest animal on Earth? (Giraffe)
  11. Which planet is closest to the Sun? (Mercury)
  12. How many minutes are in one day? (1,440)
  13. What is the chemical symbol for gold? (Au)
  14. What is the largest animal alive on Earth today? (Blue whale)
  15. What is a baby kangaroo called? (A joey)
  16. How many hearts does an octopus have? (Three)
  17. What is the capital city of Japan? (Tokyo)
  18. How many players does one soccer team have on the field at a time? (Eleven)
  19. In which direction does the Sun rise? (East)
  20. What is the only even prime number? (Two)
  21. What color do you get when you mix blue and yellow? (Green)
  22. How many keys are on a standard piano? (88)
  23. What is the capital city of Australia? (Canberra)
  24. Which gas do plants take in from the air to make food? (Carbon dioxide)
  25. At what temperature does water boil at sea level in degrees Celsius? (100 degrees)
