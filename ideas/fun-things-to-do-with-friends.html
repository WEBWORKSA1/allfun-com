70 Fun Things to Do With Friends

Friends10 min readUpdated 2026-10-29By the AllFun editors

Every friend group knows the group-chat loop: "We should hang out!" "Yes!" "What do you want to do?" "Not sure, you?" Then nothing happens for three weeks. The fix isn't more enthusiasm. It's a specific plan someone can say yes to.

So here are 70 of them, sorted into five groups: at home, out and about, cheap, adventurous and long-distance. Each idea comes with enough detail to put it into motion today. Copy one into the chat, name a date and watch how quickly people reply.

Fun things to do with friends at home

  1. Themed game night. Pick a theme (retro, trivia, cards only) and ask everyone to bring a snack that fits. Our game night ideas guide has a full playbook.
  2. Potluck cook-off. Choose one dish, like chili, tacos or cookies, and everyone brings their version. Vote anonymously for the winner.
  3. Movie bracket night. List eight movies in a genre, watch trailers, vote in head-to-head rounds and screen the champion.
  4. Mystery dinner party. Write simple character cards, hide clues around the room and solve a made-up "who took the last slice of cake?" case.
  5. Craft night. Choose one project, such as painting tote bags, potting succulents or making friendship bracelets, and buy supplies in bulk to split.
  6. Online game tournament. Take turns on one laptop playing tile merge or snake, and write high scores on a whiteboard.
  7. Lip-sync battle. Draw song titles from a bowl, give everyone ten minutes to prepare and perform in the living room.
  8. Build-a-bowl bar. Set out rice, noodles, proteins, veggies and sauces so everyone builds a custom bowl.
  9. Puzzle relay. Split into two teams with identical jigsaw puzzles and race to finish first.
  10. Karaoke night. Use lyric videos on a TV and keep a rotating song list so no one hogs the mic.
  11. Debate club for silly topics. Argue whether cereal is soup or whether socks with sandals is a valid style. Assign sides randomly with a coin flip.
  12. Spa night. Face masks, cucumber water, calm music and a nail-painting station.
  13. Learn a dance from a video. Pick one routine, practice for an hour and film the final attempt.
  14. Board game swap. Everyone brings a game the others haven't played, and you rotate through quick rounds.

Fun things to do with friends out and about

  1. Food truck tour. Visit three trucks and split one item from each.
  2. Trivia night at a café. Plenty of cafés and community spaces host quiz nights. Warm up by quizzing each other on the drive over.
  3. Bowling league for one night. Make up team names and keep a tongue-in-cheek trophy for the winner.
  4. Escape room. Book a room for your group size and see who keeps calm under the countdown.
  5. Go-kart racing. Pick a track with safety briefings and helmets included, and run a mini grand prix.
  6. Open mic as the audience. Cheer on local performers, then rate your favorite acts over dessert.
  7. Ice skating or roller skating. Hold hands, fall over and laugh about it.
  8. Climbing gym session. Many gyms offer beginner bouldering with rental shoes and an intro talk.
  9. Museum scavenger hunt. Write a list (a painting with a dog, the oldest object you can find, something blue) and split into pairs.
  10. Farmers market brunch. Each person buys one item and you assemble a picnic together.
  11. Comedy show. Find a clean comedy night or an improv show and grab dessert afterward.
  12. Arcade challenge. Pool tickets toward the single silliest prize on the shelf.
  13. Pottery or painting class. Book a group session and keep the results, no matter how lopsided.
  14. Sports game in the cheap seats. Local minor-league and college games are lively and affordable.

Cheap or free things to do with friends

  1. Picnic in the park. Everyone brings one dish, a blanket and a flying disc.
  2. Sunset hike. Choose a short trail, bring flashlights for the walk down and time it to the golden hour.
  3. Library card game afternoon. Many libraries have free community rooms or game shelves.
  4. Free outdoor events. Look for open-air concerts, movie nights or festivals in your area.
  5. Photo walk challenge. Pick a theme and compare the best shots over coffee.
  6. Bike ride to a new neighborhood. Plan a loop with a snack stop and explore streets you've never seen.
  7. Backyard or balcony campout. Tents or blankets, flashlights, ghost stories kept friendly and s'mores if you have a safe fire setup.
  8. Volunteer as a group. Help at a food bank, park cleanup or animal shelter.
  9. Would-you-rather marathon. Use our would you rather tool and keep going until someone refuses to answer.
  10. Cook from the pantry. Everyone brings two random ingredients and you invent a meal together.
  11. Garage sale treasure hunt. Give each person $5 and see who finds the best item.
  12. DIY film festival. Each person makes a one-minute movie on their phone with a shared theme, then you hold the premiere.
  13. Pickup sports. Basketball, soccer, a flying-disc game or a park badminton set.
  14. Stargazing night. Find a dark spot, bring blankets and use a free star-map app to name constellations.

Adventurous things to do with friends

  1. Weekend camping trip. Book a campsite, split the gear list and plan one big shared meal.
  2. Whitewater rafting. Choose a guided beginner trip with a licensed outfitter.
  3. Day trip to a new town. Draw a two-hour radius on a map, pick a spot none of you has visited and build the day around one landmark and one great meal.
  4. Zip-lining or ropes course. Most courses include harnesses, helmets and a safety briefing.
  5. Learn to surf or paddleboard. Book a group lesson so an instructor handles the basics.
  6. Sunrise summit. Pick a manageable peak and start early with headlamps and layers.
  7. Horseback trail ride. Choose a stable that matches riders to horses by experience level.
  8. Road trip with a theme. Visit quirky roadside attractions, diners or historic sites along one route.
  9. Cave or canyon tour. Book a guided tour so you get the history along with the views.
  10. Hot-air balloon ride. Split the cost for a dawn flight and celebrate with breakfast afterward.
  11. Fruit-picking expedition. Go to a farm and pick enough to make jam or a pie together.
  12. Snow day adventure. Sledding, snowshoeing or building the most elaborate snow sculpture possible.
  13. Train trip to a nearby city. Pack snacks, play cards on the ride and explore on foot.
  14. Overnight cabin getaway. Rent a cabin, plan one hike and leave the rest unscheduled.

Long-distance things to do with friends

  1. Video call game night. Play charades or a drawing-guessing game on camera. Send prompts by private message so only the actor sees them.
  2. Daily word game streak. Everyone plays the daily five-letter word game and posts their results in the group chat.
  3. Synchronized movie night. Press play at the same time and keep a running group-chat commentary.
  4. Virtual book club. Choose a short book, set a monthly call and take turns choosing the next pick.
  5. Cook-along. Pick a recipe, shop separately and cook together on a video call.
  6. Snail-mail swap. Send letters, postcards or a small care package with something local from your town.
  7. Online multiplayer puzzles. Work through a daily logic puzzle together, or race each other on the same sudoku grid.
  8. Shared playlist project. Each friend adds three songs a week with a short note on why.
  9. Virtual workout. Follow the same exercise video at the same time and cheer each other on.
  10. Fitness or step challenge. Track daily walks for a month and crown a winner.
  11. Plan a future trip together. Build a dream itinerary on a shared document, even if the trip is a year away.
  12. Online quiz night. Take turns hosting a trivia round with five questions each.
  13. Shared photo challenge. Post one photo a day based on a theme, like "something yellow" or "my view right now."
  14. Personality quiz comparisons. Take our fun style personality quiz and compare results in the chat.

How to actually make plans happen

  • Suggest one specific idea. "Let's do a potluck chili cook-off on Saturday at 6" gets more replies than "let's hang out."
  • Offer two date options. It's easier for everyone to pick between two than to suggest their own.
  • Let a tool break the tie. Can't decide? Put ideas on the spin wheel and let it choose.
  • Rotate the host. Spread the planning and hosting so no one burns out.
  • Make it recurring. A standing first-Friday game night takes the planning out of the equation.

Frequently asked questions

What can I do with friends when there's nothing to do?

Pick one concrete idea and commit to it: a potluck cook-off, a park picnic, a game night or a photo walk. Having a specific plan with a time is what turns "nothing to do" into a great evening.

What are cheap things to do with friends?

Picnics, sunset hikes, free outdoor events, pickup sports, pantry cook-offs, game nights and group volunteering all cost little or nothing. Splitting supplies for a craft night or a potluck also keeps costs low.

How can I stay close to long-distance friends?

Set up a regular activity you can share, such as a monthly video call game night, a shared playlist, a virtual book club or a daily word game streak. Small, consistent check-ins matter more than big occasional ones.

What are fun things to do with friends at night?

Stargazing, karaoke, game nights, mystery dinners, movie brackets, trivia nights and backyard campouts are all great evening options. Choose something that fits your group's energy level and how late everyone wants to stay out.

How do you plan a hangout with a big group of friends?

Pick an activity that scales, like a potluck, a park picnic or bowling. Share a date and time early, assign simple jobs (snacks, games, drinks, cleanup) and keep the plan flexible so people can join late or leave early without fuss.
